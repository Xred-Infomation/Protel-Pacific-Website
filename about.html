Company Profile & History

Founded in 1991, Protel Pacific is a dedicated CMS (Contract Manufacturing Services) provider, offering precision metal fabrication, plastic injection, electronic PCB assembly, and complete product integration.

Originally establishing our manufacturing roots through high-standard telecommunication and payphone assemblies for Protel Inc. USA, we have expanded our comprehensive engineering capabilities across HVAC, commercial lighting, and electronics industries worldwide.

Headquartered in Taipei, Taiwan with our primary manufacturing plant situated in Dong Guan, China (Dong Guan Tian Sheng Electronic Production Corp.), our facility spans over 20,000 square meters and is operated by a dedicated workforce of over 300 skilled professionals.
