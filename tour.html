Main Factory Facility

20,000 m² modern manufacturing plant in Dong Guan, China.

Luminaire Assembly Line

Standardized flow line with ESD protection and in-line testing.

Samsung SMT Placement

Supports micro 0201 chips, BGA packages, and high-density LED arrays.

Wave Soldering Machine

Dual-wave lead-free soldering ensuring robust through-hole connections.

CNC Milling Center

Multi-axis high-speed machining for aluminum heatsinks and housings.

Fiber Laser Cutting

High precision cutting for stainless steel and aluminum sheet metals.

GO1900 Goniophotometer

Measures spatial light intensity distribution and generates IES test data.

Coordinate Measuring (CMM)

Precise verification of dimensional tolerances and mold geometry.

Salt Spray Test Chamber

Validates anti-corrosion performance for surface powder coatings.

Schleich Electrical Tester

100% dielectric withstand, insulation resistance, and ground continuity testing.

Glow Wire Flammability Tester

Ensures plastic housings comply with UL94 fire safety standards.

Transportation Vibration Tester

Simulates rough transit conditions to ensure packaging integrity.
